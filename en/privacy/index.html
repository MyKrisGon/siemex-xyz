02What we collect

In the access and audit form

  • Name, business name and type, city, country, WhatsApp, email (optional), website or social media profile (optional) and what you're interested in (the app, the audit or both).
  • Public information about your business that we review for the audit (website, Google profile, social media).
  • Where you came from (for example, an ad) and how you use the site (pages viewed and whether you sent the form), measured with Google Analytics.

In the Small Business Study (survey and interviews, closed November 1, 2026)

  • Contact details, only if you request your audit: name, business name, email, phone or WhatsApp (optional) and your website or main social media profile.
  • About your business: industry, city, state or province, country, years in business, team size and your role.
  • The channels and tools you use, your approximate monthly ad spend, and your opinions and challenges.
  • In interviews you agree to: notes from the conversation and, only with your explicit permission at the start, a recording.
  • Public information about your business that we review for the audit (website, Google profile, social media).

In the app (plataforma.siemex.xyz)

  • Your account: name, email, password (stored encrypted; nobody can see it) and preferred language.
  • Your business details: name, industry, city, country, website and business phone or WhatsApp.
  • Numbers from the accounts you connect (website, Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram and Google ads): daily totals such as visits, times you showed up on Google, calls, direction requests, ad spend, clicks and number of conversations. Access to those accounts is read-only and you can remove it at any time.
  • WhatsApp messages, only if you connect your number: so you can read and reply from the app, we store the conversations of your WhatsApp Business number: the profile name and number of whoever writes to you, the text of the messages, the automatic replies you set up, which ad each conversation came from, and their answers to the assistant's questions. Photos, audio and documents aren't stored; only a note that they arrived. For these conversations your business is the controller of its customers' data and SIEMEX processes it only on your behalf, as a processor, to provide the service: we don't use it for anything else, we don't sell it and we don't contact your customers.
  • What you do in the app: your decisions on recommendations, your service requests, the prices you approve and the messages you send us.
  • Technical security data: sign-in dates and times and the IP address of requests, to prevent abuse.

We don't collect sensitive information such as health, ethnicity, religion or sexual orientation. Please don't include it in open-ended answers or messages.

03How we use it

Required purposes

  • Analyze study answers, always in aggregate.
  • Prepare and send your free audit, and contact you for the call, if you asked for them.
  • Invite you to be one of the first users of the app, if you asked.
  • Give you access to the app, show you your dashboard and prepare recommendations.
  • If you connect WhatsApp: receive and send your business's messages, send the automatic replies you set up and show you the conversations.
  • Handle your service requests: review them, send you a price, do the work and keep you updated.
  • Issue invoices when you hire a service.
  • Keep the app and your account secure.

Optional

  • Send you news and offers from SIEMEX by email or text message.

We only use your information for the optional purpose if you agree to it separately (the survey and the access form have their own checkbox). You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing us or replying to any message; it doesn't affect your audit or your use of the app.

We never publish your business's name, your numbers or your individual answers without your written permission. Any study results we share will be percentages and trends across all participants.

05How long we keep it

  • Access and audit requests: up to 24 months after your last interaction with us, or sooner if you ask us to delete them. If you join the app, the app's period applies.
  • Study contacts: up to 24 months after the study ends, or sooner if you ask us to delete them. After that, answers are kept only in anonymous form.
  • Interview recordings: deleted no later than 6 months after the interview.
  • Your app account and its data: while your account is active. If you close it, we delete it within 90 days, except invoices for services you hired, which we keep for 5 years for tax purposes.
  • WhatsApp conversations: while your number is connected. If you disconnect it or close your account, we delete them within 90 days. You can ask us to delete a conversation sooner.
  • Technical security data: up to 12 months.

06Cookies and browser storage

siemex.xyz uses Google Analytics cookies to measure visits, where visitors come from and whether the form is sent. It doesn't use advertising cookies. You can block them in your browser or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on; the site works the same. More in Google's privacy policy.

The app uses a single, essential cookie to keep you signed in (it lasts 7 days) and saves your chosen language in your browser. It doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

If you messaged a business that uses SIEMEX on WhatsApp and want your conversation deleted, you can ask that business or ask us directly the same way.
